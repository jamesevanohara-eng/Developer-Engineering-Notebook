02 — Revamping the About Me Page

Date: August 20, 2026

Goal

My goal was to improve the design of my About Me page and make it look more modern while keeping the overall style consistent with the rest of my website.

What I Asked AI

I asked AI to help redesign my About Me page, particularly the layout, image placement, spacing, colors, and overall visual appearance.

My Prompt

Revamp my About Me page and make it look more modern and polished. Keep the style consistent with the other pages of my website. Make the image smaller and aligned on the right instead of taking up too much space. Improve the layout, spacing, colors, and overall design while keeping the page easy to read.

AI Response

AI provided an updated version of my HTML and CSS that reorganized the About Me section and changed the image placement. The image was moved to the right side of the content and reduced in size. AI also suggested improvements to spacing, backgrounds, borders, typography, and other visual elements so the page looked more professional.

What I Actually Did

I used the suggested code as a starting point and added it to my website. I tested the page and adjusted the design when parts of the layout did not look the way I wanted. I specifically focused on making the image smaller and properly aligned with the rest of the content.

Was the AI Correct?

Mostly. The code worked, but some of the visual changes did not initially match what I wanted. I had to give AI additional instructions about the image position, size, and how the different sections should fit together.

What I Learned

I learned that AI-generated code may work technically while still not matching the design I have in mind. Giving specific instructions and testing the result is important when using AI for web development.

My Takeaway

AI can help me redesign and improve my website, but I still need to evaluate the result and communicate exactly what I want changed.

Next AI Experiment

My next experiment will be using AI to help me add more JavaScript interactions to my website. I want to understand how the JavaScript works instead of relying entirely on AI to write it for me.
