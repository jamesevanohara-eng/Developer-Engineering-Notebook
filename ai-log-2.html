02 — Revamping the About Me Page

Date: August 20, 2026

Goal

My goal was to use AI to improve the design and layout of my About Me page while keeping it consistent with the rest of my website.

What I Asked AI

Prompt:

Revamp this About Me page. Better, but once again the photo isn't aligned just focus on that for now. Like I said I don't like how different sections are separated. For instance keep the style similar to the other pages, here's an example.

I then provided AI with my existing About Me HTML and an example of another page from my website.

What I Actually Did

I used the AI-generated code to update my About Me page and tested the changes on my website. I specifically focused on improving the photo's alignment and making the different sections fit together more naturally.

Was the AI Correct?

The code was mostly correct and gave me a good starting point. However, I had to continue giving AI feedback because the image alignment and spacing were not exactly what I wanted at first.

What I Learned

I learned that AI can quickly generate and reorganize HTML, but I still need to test the result and explain what I want changed. Small design details such as image alignment and spacing can require multiple revisions.

My Takeaway

AI can help me build and improve a website much faster, but I still need to evaluate the code and make sure the final result matches my goals.
